How We Accelerate VLSI Engineering Careers in India

A structured, tape-out-driven pathway bridging foundational silicon concepts to core industry deployment across India.

01

RTL / FPGA Design

We deliver high-performance RTL design and FPGA development solutions, translating complex system specifications into robust, synthesizable digital architectures. Our focus is on design efficiency, scalability, and optimized power, performance, and area (PPA).

02

Design Verification / SoC Verification

We provide comprehensive IP, block-level, and SoC verification services to ensure functional correctness and design robustness. Leveraging advanced verification methodologies, we help achieve high functional coverage, early bug detection, and first-time-right silicon.

03

Design for Testability (DFT)

We offer end-to-end DFT solutions that enhance chip testability, improve manufacturing yield, and ensure product reliability. Our expertise includes scan insertion, ATPG, MBIST, and test coverage optimization for production-ready silicon.

04

Physical Design

We provide end-to-end physical design and timing closure solutions, covering floorplanning, placement, clock tree synthesis, routing, and sign-off analysis. Our methodologies optimize PPA while ensuring timing convergence and implementation readiness.

05

Physical Verification

We deliver precision-driven physical verification services to ensure layout integrity and compliance with foundry design rules. Our expertise includes DRC, LVS, ERC, and sign-off verification to support reliable, manufacturable, and tape-out-ready designs.

06

Analog Circuit Design / Analog Layout Design

We specialize in analog and mixed-signal circuit design, developing precision-engineered solutions tailored to demanding performance requirements. Our analog layout expertise emphasizes device matching, parasitic control, noise minimization, and layout optimization for robust silicon performance.

07

Advanced STA & Timing Closure

We provide timing analysis and closure support to ensure reliable chip performance across operating conditions. Our expertise includes setup and hold analysis, timing constraints validation, MCMM analysis, clock skew evaluation, and timing optimization to achieve design frequency targets.

08

EMIR Analysis

We support power integrity analysis to evaluate electromigration (EM) and IR-drop risks across the power delivery network. Our focus includes static and dynamic IR-drop analysis, current-density evaluation, power-grid integrity, and identifying potential reliability issues to support robust silicon implementation.
